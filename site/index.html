Your AI command center. On your own hardware.

OMNIX is a local-first AI desktop assistant. Chat, memory, voice and system control all run on your machine and your GPUs, and your data stays on your disk.

Get OMNIX See what it does

More than a chat box. A workspace.

OMNIX combines a local model, long-term memory and your machine's live state in one place, so the assistant knows your context and can act on it with your approval.

🖥️

Hardware-native

Live CPU, memory and per-GPU monitoring for NVIDIA and AMD, with VRAM, temperature and power, plus which models are loaded and whether they fit on the GPU.

🧠

Persistent memory

Hybrid keyword and semantic search over your memories and notes, with recency, reinforcement and corrections. Point it at an Obsidian vault and it keeps it indexed.

📋

Situation briefs

One click summarises what's happening on your system: alerts, load, services and recent activity.

🎙️

Voice, locally

Push-to-talk with local speech-to-text and read-aloud through local voices. No audio leaves the machine.

⚡

Commands with guardrails

Shell, file and system actions are risk-classified. Anything that changes your system needs your approval in a native dialog, and every action lands in a tamper-evident audit log.

📊

See your own ROI

A private usage ledger shows the turns, tokens and tool calls your local models handled, and what the same work would have cost from a cloud API.

Privacy isn't a feature. It's the default.

Most AI tools send every prompt to someone else's server. OMNIX starts in local-only mode and enforces it in the backend, not just in the interface.

  • Local-only by default. Cloud providers are blocked until you turn them on.
  • Keys in your OS keychain. They're never stored in settings files or shown again.
  • Encrypted memory at rest (SQLCipher) as an option, with the key in your keyring.
  • Air-gap friendly. Licenses are checked offline, and nothing phones home.
  • Hash-chained audit log of every sensitive action.
