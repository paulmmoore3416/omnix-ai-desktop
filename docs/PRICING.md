## Where OMNIX sits

OMNIX covers more ground than a chat front-end: system control with native approvals, NVIDIA and AMD
monitoring, an audit log, long-term memory, voice and automations. That puts it in the BoltAI and Msty class, not
the TypingMind class. But it is new and unproven, it is Linux-first, and free tools (LM Studio, Jan, AnythingLLM)
cover the basics. So the launch price sits at the market median for one-time desktop licenses, below Msty.

## OMNIX list prices

| Edition | Price | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Community** | free | Matches the free local runners, so people can try OMNIX without a card. |
| **Lifetime Pro** | **$99 one-time** (launch offer **$79**); 2 devices; all v1.x updates | Same as BoltAI Pro and TypingMind Premium, well under Msty's $349. The $79 launch price matches BoltAI Essential. |
| **BYOK** | **$10 / month** or **$96 / year** | Same as Raycast Pro ($10, or $8 annually), under Msty's ≈ $12.40. API usage is billed by the provider, never by OMNIX. |
| **Enterprise Hardened** | **from $4,800 / year**, which covers up to 20 seats ($20 / seat / month); extra seats $20 / seat / month; optional air-gapped deployment and hardening package from **$2,500** one-time | Per seat, this is between Open WebUI's license-only rate (≈ $8 / user / month) and Tabnine's $39–59. It includes support and a site-level hardening review, which regulated buyers (healthcare, legal, finance) usually expect to pay for. "Outcome-based" terms are negotiated on top. |

Suggested policies, in line with the market:

- A 30-day money-back guarantee, as BoltAI offers.
- Education and non-profit discount of 50 %, as BoltAI offers.
- Price protection: a Lifetime license never pays again for v1.x.
- A major version (v2) may be a paid upgrade at about 50 % of list, which is the norm for perpetual licenses.

Review these prices after the first 100 sales, or sooner if conversion from Community is under about 2 %.
